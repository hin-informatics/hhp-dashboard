## 5. Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Optimisation Refinement

### Clinical Rationale
* Under **NICE NG203 (1.6.1) & CG182**, patients with normal/mild albuminuria ($< 3\text{ mg/mmol}$ with T2D, or $< 22.6\text{ mg/mmol}$ without T2D) do not require ACEi/ARB or SGLT2i for renal protection, but **Atorvastatin 20mg** is recommended for all CKD stages 1–5 for CVD prevention.
* The legacy code required elevated ACR ($\ge 3$ or $\ge 22.6$) to qualify for optimisation, inadvertently classifying 157 patients (71.7% of CKD cohort) as unoptimised.

### Modified
* **[`scripts/func_optimisation.R`](file:///C:/Users/k2481754/OneDrive%20-%20King's%20College%20London/Documents/My%20Projects/HHP/hhp-dashboard/scripts/func_optimisation.R)**:
  * **High ACR ($\ge 3$ with T2D, $\ge 22.6$ without T2D):** Requires ACEi/ARB + SGLT2i + Statin (or documented exceptions).
  * **Normal / Mild ACR ($< 3$ with T2D, $< 22.6$ without T2D):** Optimised if on **Statin** (`statin_ok == TRUE`).
  * **Optimisation Date:** `opt_date_ckd` dynamically calculates based on high vs. low ACR and is capped at `dx_date_ckd` to prevent negative turnaround days from pre-diagnostic statin prescriptions.
  * **Outcome:** CKD optimisation increased from **1.4% (3 patients)** to **34.7% (76 patients)**.

## 6. Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) Optimisation Refinement (Rule 1: Dual Therapy with Exceptions)

### Clinical Rationale
* Enforced that all T2D patients must be on active pharmacotherapy (or have documented exceptions) in addition to glycemic control, disallowing diet/lifestyle-only management per February clinical guidelines.
* Reverted to **Rule 1**:
  * **Non-frail T2D:** Requires **Current Metformin (or exception) AND Current SGLT2i (or exception) AND $\text{HbA1c} \le 53\text{ mmol/mol}$**.
  * **Frail T2D:** Requires **Current Metformin (or exception) AND $\text{HbA1c} \le 75\text{ mmol/mol}$**.

### Modified
* **[`scripts/func_optimisation.R`](file:///C:/Users/k2481754/OneDrive%20-%20King's%20College%20London/Documents/My%20Projects/HHP/hhp-dashboard/scripts/func_optimisation.R)**:
  * Implemented Rule 1 dual therapy logic with `metformin_ok & sglt2i_ok & hb_a1c_value <= 53`.
  * Preserved full clinical exception handling (`diabetic_med_excluded == 1` and `sglt2i_excluded == 1`).
  * Reverted `opt_date_t2d` to the max issue date across Metformin, SGLT2i, and HbA1c, capped at `dx_date_t2d` to guarantee non-negative turnaround times.
  * **Outcome:** T2D optimisation is **3.7% (5 / 135 patients)**.

## 8. Diabetes HbA1c Optimisation Threshold Update (58 mmol/mol)
